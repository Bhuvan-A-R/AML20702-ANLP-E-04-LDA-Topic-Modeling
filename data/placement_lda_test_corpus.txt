The placement office used a structured information extraction system to organize recruitment narratives. The system recognized PERSON, ORGANIZATION, LOCATION, DATE, DEPARTMENT, COMPANY, STUDENT_NAME, USN and PACKAGE entities. Regular expressions detected university numbers and salary values such as 8 LPA, 12 LPA, 18 LPA and 24 LPA. Sentence level co occurrence created relations between students, departments, companies and packages. Date parsing converted expressions into ISO dates for chronological event timelines.

The information extraction pipeline identified recruitment actions such as conducted, visited, recruited, selected, offered, joined and verified. Each event was linked to a sentence and a normalized date. The resulting timeline showed a placement drive on 15th January 2024, a company visit on 20th April 2024 and an offer verification event on 2nd May 2024. The dashboard displayed event order, company, student, department, package and source sentence for every extracted record.

The LDA topic modelling experiment transformed cleaned placement narratives into token lists and a Gensim dictionary. Stopwords, short tokens, punctuation and duplicate documents were removed before the bag of words corpus was created. The model learned topic word probabilities using online variational Bayes. Topic coherence and perplexity were calculated after training. Interactive visualizations showed topic prevalence, frequent words, topic word heatmaps and document topic probability distributions.

The final placement report combined structured tables, charts and explanatory observations. It presented dataset statistics, preprocessing results, named entity counts, relationship examples and chronological events. The knowledge graph connected students to companies, departments and salary packages. The timeline ordered recruitment activities by parsed date. The report also described limitations such as ambiguous names, incomplete dates, overlapping entities, small datasets, heuristic relation extraction and the need for human validation before using automated results.
